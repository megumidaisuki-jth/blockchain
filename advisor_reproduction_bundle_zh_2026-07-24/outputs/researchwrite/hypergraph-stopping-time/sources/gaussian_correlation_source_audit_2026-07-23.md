# Gaussian correlation source audit

- Verified primary theorem source: Thomas Royen (2014), *A Simple Proof of the Gaussian Correlation Conjecture Extended to Some Multivariate Gamma Distributions*, *Far East Journal of Theoretical Statistics* 48(2), 139–145.
- Preprint identifier: arXiv:1408.1028.
- The theorem used here is the centered-Gaussian, symmetric-convex-set correlation inequality.
- Project use: finite time-grid channel survival sets are symmetric convex; repeated two-set application yields the multi-channel product lower bound.
- Boundary qualification: continuous-time passage uses closed safe sets first; equivalence to first-touch survival requires no atom at the boundary supremum.
- Not claimed: a nonsymmetric-set inequality, a drifted comparison theorem, or a direct theorem for non-binary hyperedge simplices.
